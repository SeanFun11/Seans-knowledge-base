# Building the Core Menu
Building a core menu can be difficult because it causes you too look outside the box but also in it. It makes you have to go and learn from the cuisine your interested in and others outside of your plan for deeper thought on final menu options. For instance, if I was to do Mac and cheese I would have to first figure out my base and what I wanted it to be taste like and look like. Then I would move on to toppings what style of Mac & Cheese? Would I do BBQ, Seafood, Soul food, or many other options. I will use BBQ for my example and do a brisket Mac and cheese.  Now I have to come up with the style of brisket the seasoning. How do I want to make my BBQ sauce? What will the BBQ  base be? How can I bring to ether all three textures and flavors to work in harmony. 

## Why Mac and Cheese 
I grew up on Mac and cheese and I came to Love Brisket when I moved to Texas so one day I threw them together and Bam!
## Deciding How Many Items 
I couldn't Just Have Mac and cheese so I would add any where from 6-8 items to the menu to keep it condensed for a food truck setting. Due to limited space. 
### What Got Cut 
i had to take out seafood in my model because the food cost did not make sense on the long run. 
#### The One That Still Bothers Me 
What bothers me the most is the fact that stuff breaks so much that I will have to factor that into my business model as well.
