# Costing a Dish The Brisket Mac 

## The Ingredients 
Noodles, cheese, brisket, BBQ sauce

## Working Out the Price 
1. 16oz serving size 
2.  The ram ingredients cost is $6.00
3. Container and fork cost would be. .35 cents
4.  I mark up about three times the ingredients cost for labor and industry standards. 
5. which would bring the final Price to about $18
## What the Number Doesn't Show Labor, propane, commissary fees, the stuff that never lands in the food cost but comes out of the same pocket.
