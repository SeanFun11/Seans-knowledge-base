A **commissary** is a licensed commercial kitchen where food trucks prepare food, store supplies, and service their vehicles. Many health departments require food trucks to operate from a commissary.

## What a Commissary Provides

- **Prep space** for chopping, marinating, and cooking large batches
- **Cold and dry storage** for ingredients
- **Fresh water** for filling the truck's tanks
- **Wastewater and grease disposal**
- **Parking** for the truck, sometimes with electrical hookups

## Why It's Required

A truck's kitchen is small, and it can't safely store or prep everything on its own. The commissary gives the health department a fixed, inspectable location tied to your truck (see [[health-inspections|Health Inspections]]).

## Planning Prep Work

Doing as much as possible at the commissary makes service faster.

| Prep at the Commissary | Finish on the Truck |
|---|---|
| Chop vegetables | Grill or fry to order |
| Make sauces and dressings | Assemble sandwiches and bowls |
| Portion meats | Add fresh toppings |
| Cook slow items like braises | Reheat to safe temperature |

> [!tip] Use a prep list
> Write a daily prep list based on expected sales. It keeps the crew organized and prevents running out of key items mid-rush.

## Safe Prep Practices

- Cool cooked food **quickly** before storing it
- **Label** containers with the item name and date
- Transport food in **insulated or temperature-controlled** containers
- Reheat food to the required temperature (see [[food-safety-basics|Food Safety Basics]])
