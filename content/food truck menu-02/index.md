This section covers how a food truck actually runs: how the menu is designed, how food is cooked and held safely, how a dish is priced, how service is managed at the window, and how specials keep the offering fresh. It also covers where ingredients come from, how prep works at a commissary, and the food safety rules every menu has to follow.

## How These Pages Fit Together

Menu design sets the limits, holding and service determine whether the menu is achievable, costing decides whether it is profitable, and specials feed new ideas back into the menu. Sourcing, commissary prep, and food safety are the foundation underneath all of it. Each page links to the others where the topics overlap.
