## Purpose

Food trucks look simple from the sidewalk, but behind the window is a small business juggling permits, food safety, equipment, menus, and marketing all at once. Information on these topics is often scattered across government sites, forums, and vendor blogs. This knowledge base pulls the essentials together into **one organized, plain-language guide**.

## Who It's For

- **Aspiring owners** researching whether a food truck is the right move
- **New crew members** learning how a truck operates day to day
- **Students** studying small business, hospitality, or entrepreneurship

## About the Author

This knowledge base was created by **Sean Funches** as a course project. Sean has worked as a line cook and has hands-on experience in mobile food service, which shaped the practical focus of this guide.

> [!note] A note on accuracy
> Rules for food trucks vary by city, county, and state. Use this site as a starting point, and always confirm requirements with your local health department, fire marshal, and city offices.
