This page lists the outside sources used to research this knowledge base. Each source links to the pages where it is used.

> [!note]
> Food truck rules vary by location. These sources provide general guidance; always confirm requirements with your local health department, fire marshal, and city offices.

## Government Sources

### U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)
**[FDA Food Code](https://www.fda.gov/food/retail-food-protection/fda-food-code)**
The model food safety code that state and local health departments adopt for restaurants and mobile food units.
- Used in: [[food-safety-basics|Food Safety Basics]], [[health-inspections|Health Inspections]], [[prep-and-commissary|Prep and Commissary]]

### FoodSafety.gov
**[4 Steps to Food Safety](https://www.foodsafety.gov/keep-food-safe/4-steps-to-food-safety)**
The clean, separate, cook, and chill guidelines for safe food handling.
- Used in: [[food-safety-basics|Food Safety Basics]]

### U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA)
**[Write Your Business Plan](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/write-your-business-plan)**
The standard sections of a traditional business plan.
- Used in: [[business-plan|Writing a Business Plan]]

**[Market Research and Competitive Analysis](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/market-research-competitive-analysis)**
How to study customers and competitors before launching.
- Used in: [[choosing-a-concept|Choosing a Concept]], [[finding-locations|Finding Locations]]

**[Calculate Your Startup Costs](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/calculate-your-startup-costs)**
How to identify and estimate the costs of opening a business.
- Used in: [[startup-costs|Startup Costs]]

**[Apply for Licenses and Permits](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/launch-your-business/apply-licenses-permits)**
Federal, state, and local licensing requirements for small businesses.
- Used in: [[permits-and-licenses|Permits and Licenses]]

### Internal Revenue Service (IRS)
**[Small Business and Self-Employed Tax Center](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed)**
Guidance on EINs, recordkeeping, and business taxes.
- Used in: [[permits-and-licenses|Permits and Licenses]], [[point-of-sale-and-payments|Point of Sale and Payments]]

## History Sources

### Atlas Obscura
**[Before Food Trucks, Americans Ate 'Night Lunch' From Beautiful Wagons](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/night-lunch-wagon)**
The story of Walter Scott's 1872 lunch wagon in Providence and how lunch wagons led to diners.
- Used in: [[history-of-food-trucks|History of Food Trucks]]

### Wikipedia
**[Food truck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_truck)**
Overview of food truck history, including Charles Goodnight's 1866 chuckwagon and the rise of gourmet trucks.
- Used in: [[history-of-food-trucks|History of Food Trucks]]

## Safety Organizations

### National Fire Protection Association (NFPA)
**[Food Truck Safety](https://www.nfpa.org/education-and-research/building-and-life-safety/food-truck-safety)**
Fire, propane, and generator safety requirements for mobile cooking operations, including the NFPA food truck safety fact sheet.
- Used in: [[fire-and-propane-safety|Fire and Propane Safety]], [[kitchen-layout|Kitchen Layout]], [[truck-maintenance|Truck Maintenance]], [[daily-operations|Daily Operations Checklist]]
