A **point-of-sale (POS) system** is the tablet, card reader, and software used to take orders and payments. On a food truck, a fast POS keeps the line moving.

## Key POS Features

- Accepts **credit cards, debit cards, and mobile wallets** (tap to pay)
- Works on **mobile data**, with an offline mode for weak signals
- Sends orders to the kitchen, by printer or screen
- Tracks **sales reports** by item, day, and location
- Calculates **sales tax** automatically
- Supports **online ordering** or loyalty programs (optional)

## Card vs. Cash

| | Card and Mobile Payments | Cash |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Fast with tap to pay | Slower when making change |
| **Fees** | Processing fee on each sale | No fee |
| **Safety** | Less money on the truck | Risk of theft or loss |
| **Hygiene** | Less handling | Money must be kept away from food |

Many trucks accept both, since some customers still prefer cash.

> [!tip] Use your sales data
> POS reports show which items sell best and which locations make the most money. Use them when updating your [[building-a-core-menu|menu]] and [[finding-locations|route]].

## Keeping Records

Good records make taxes and planning much easier:
- Save daily sales reports
- Track tips separately
- Keep receipts for business expenses
- Reconcile POS deposits with your bank account

The IRS provides guidance for small business owners on recordkeeping and taxes (see [[references|References]]).
