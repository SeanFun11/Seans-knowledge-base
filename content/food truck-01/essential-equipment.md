The exact equipment depends on the menu (a coffee truck and a barbecue truck look nothing alike), but most food trucks share the same core systems.

## Cooking Equipment

Choose based on your [[building-a-core-menu|menu]]:
- **Flat-top griddle:** Burgers, cheesesteaks, breakfast items
- **Deep fryer:** Fries, wings, fried sides
- **Range or burners:** Sauces, pasta, soups
- **Oven:** Baked goods, pizzas, or holding food warm

## Refrigeration and Holding

| Equipment | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Reach-in or under-counter refrigerator | Keeps ingredients cold near the line |
| Freezer | Stores frozen items |
| Prep table with cold rail | Keeps toppings cold while assembling orders |
| Hot holding unit or steam table | Keeps cooked food at safe temperatures |

## Water and Sanitation

- **Three-compartment sink** for washing, rinsing, and sanitizing
- **Separate handwashing sink**
- **Fresh water tank** and a larger **wastewater tank**
- **Water heater** and **water pump**

## Power and Safety

- **Generator** or electrical hookup
- **Propane tanks** and secure mounting
- **Ventilation hood** with exhaust fan
- **Fire suppression system** and **fire extinguishers** (see [[fire-and-propane-safety|Fire and Propane Safety]])

## Small but Important

- Food thermometers
- Cutting boards in separate colors for raw and ready-to-eat foods
- Food storage containers with lids
- Point-of-sale device (see [[point-of-sale-and-payments|Point of Sale and Payments]])

> [!tip] Buy for the menu you have
> It's tempting to install every appliance "just in case." Extra equipment costs money, uses power, and takes up space you need for workflow.
