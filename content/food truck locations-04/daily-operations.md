A smooth service day comes from routines. This checklist brings together steps from across the knowledge base into one daily flow.

## Before Leaving the Commissary

- [ ] Complete the prep list (see [[prep-and-commissary|Prep and Commissary]])
- [ ] Load food in temperature-controlled containers
- [ ] Fill the fresh water tank; confirm the wastewater tank is empty
- [ ] Check propane levels and connections (see [[fire-and-propane-safety|Fire and Propane Safety]])
- [ ] Stock packaging, napkins, and cleaning supplies
- [ ] Confirm the POS device is charged
- [ ] Post today's location on social media (see [[social-media-marketing|Social Media Marketing]])
- [ ] Do a quick vehicle check: tires, lights, fluids

## Setting Up On Site

1. Park with proper **clearance** from buildings and vehicles
2. Level the truck and secure it
3. Start the generator with exhaust pointed away from customers
4. Turn on and preheat cooking equipment
5. Check refrigerator and hot-holding temperatures
6. Stock the handwashing sink
7. Put out the menu board and open the window

## During Service

- Check food temperatures regularly and **log** them
- Keep the handwashing sink accessible
- Restock the line during slow moments
- Note any items that sell out

## Closing Down

1. Turn off cooking equipment and propane
2. Store leftover food safely or discard it according to food safety rules
3. Clean all surfaces and equipment
4. Secure all items for driving
5. Close out the POS and count any cash

## Back at the Commissary

- [ ] Dump wastewater and grease properly
- [ ] Unload and store food
- [ ] Review sales and write tomorrow's prep list
- [ ] Note any maintenance needs (see [[truck-maintenance|Truck Maintenance]])

> [!tip] Make it your own
> Print this list and laminate it for the truck. Adjust it to fit your menu, equipment, and local rules.
